Training Hub Guide

How to navigate and use the Training Hub: follow these steps to get the most out of your training.

  1. 1

    Log in

    Use the email and password your administrator gave you on the login page. Accounts are created by your administrator, so there is no sign-up. Forgot your password? Ask your administrator to reset it.

  2. 2

    Start with About Us

    Open About Us before anything else and play the team video at normal speed (skipping ahead and fast-forwarding are turned off). Once you reach 50% of the video, the Next button unlocks and takes you to Module 1. Your spot is saved, so you can pick up where you left off.

  3. 3

    Start with the Modules

    Once you have watched 50% of About Us, open Modules in the top menu. There are five required modules, Module 01 to Module 05, plus an optional Refresher (Module 06). Work through them in order at your own pace. When you are 50% through a module, its Next Module button unlocks.

  4. 4

    Learn, then take the quiz

    Read or watch each lesson, then answer the knowledge checks and quizzes. Inside a module, the Next button (and any jump ahead) stays locked until you have read at least half of the current page, so take your time. Your scores and the time you spend are recorded automatically. The pass mark is 80% unless your administrator changes it.

  5. 5

    Use the progress box

    On a module page, the box in the bottom-right corner shows your status. A module is flagged Completed automatically once your Completion Rate reaches 100% and your assessment score meets the pass mark. For an assessment on a Google Form, enter your result with Log score.

  6. 6

    Check My Progress

    Click My Progress in the top menu to see your About Us status, modules completed, your best score, every quiz attempt, and whether you are certified. Only you and your administrator can see it.

  7. 7

    Earn your certification

    Complete Modules 01 to 04 and pass the final assessment in Module 05 (80% or higher) to earn your certification badge. Module 06 is optional: come back to it for refreshers on policy updates.

  8. 8

    Log out when you are done

    Click the Logout button at the top right, especially on a shared computer. Need help or access? Ask your administrator.
